Review Findings

8. Party, project, method, and protocol metadata is reduced.
Missing content includes addresses, phones, URLs and positions (veg-misc.xsd:67-82); project personnel, funding, study area, design, related projects and citations (veg-misc.xsd:106-136); and method subjects and protocol grouping (veg-misc.xsd:490, veg-misc.xsd:517). JSON does add ORCID and richer web citations.

9. Community classifications lose curation detail.
Community rank and common name are absent, as are stratum-specific determinations, fit, confidence, and assertion authority (veg-community.xsd:14-16, veg-community.xsd:32-79). JSON retains concept reference, determiner, date, and notes (vegx.schema.json:485).

10. Controlled definitions are partly flattened.
Stratum definitions and observed stratum properties now share one object; surface-type definitions are replaced with free-text values. Generic attributes can carry much of the actual data, but the distinction between vocabulary definition and observation is weakened.

11. Note and identifier metadata is lost.
XSD notes have author, date, and category; JSON notes are plain strings. XSD identifiers also carry system and document/global scope, while JSON IDs do not.

12. JSON rejects datasets without observations.
The XSD permits metadata-only or empty datasets; JSON requires at least one plotObservation through required plus minItems: 1.
